How Sea Temperature Affects Coral

Coral reefs are highly sensitive to changes in sea surface temperature. When water temperatures rise above the normal range, corals become stressed and may expel the symbiotic algae that provide them with energy and color.

As temperature stress increases, coral bleaching becomes more severe. Prolonged exposure to high temperatures can weaken corals and increase the risk of mortality.
